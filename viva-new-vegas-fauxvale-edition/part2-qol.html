Part Two - Quality of Life

With stability sorted, Part Two is a grab-bag of small comforts - mods that make Fallout: New Vegas a little nicer to play.

!

Everything in this section is optional. Install all of them, a few, or none at all - each one stands on its own, and skipping any won't break anything.

How to install each one

Same flow as OneTweak in Part One: on the mod's Nexus page, open the Files tab and click Mod Manager Download, let it arrive in MO2's Downloads pane, double-click it to install (keep the default name), then drag it under your Fauxvale Edition separator and tick its checkbox to activate. Repeat for whichever mods you choose.

  1. Better Character Creation

    Optional

    Improves character creation by speeding up the process, adding specialized gear based on your tag skills, and making Wild Wasteland an opt-in feature rather than a trait.

    Open on Nexus ↗

  2. Less Clean Vanilla HUD - Health & AP Bar Tick Tweak

    Optional

    Adds a subtle fade/gradient to the health and AP ticks on the HUD with Clean Vanilla HUD, making it closer to the vanilla look.

    Open on Nexus ↗

  3. Enhanced FOV - ESPless

    Optional

    An in-game FOV switcher - corrected broken weapon zoom, scope switching, separate 1st/3rd person FOVs, and more!

    Grab both main files. Download the mod itself and its accompanying INI. Install them as two separate mods, and place the INI mod directly below the main Enhanced FOV mod under your Fauxvale Edition separator.
    Open on Nexus ↗

  4. Slideshow FOV Fix

    Only if you change FOV

    Handles the "zoom" issue with slideshow sizing on custom FOV settings.

    Only needed if you plan to change your FOV. If you skipped Enhanced FOV and are keeping the default field of view, you can safely skip this one too.
    Open on Nexus ↗
