Step 01 - Foundation

Install Base
Viva New Vegas

Before Fauxvale Edition can layer on top, you need a complete, working Viva New Vegas install. You won't do that here - you'll do it on the official VNV guide, then come back to this terminal for the next steps. Pick one of the three routes below. We recommend Wabbajack, ideally with Nexus Premium.

▸

Recommendation: Use Wabbajack with Nexus Premium for the fastest, most hands-off install. Premium isn't required - Wabbajack works fine without it, you'll just click each download by hand. Prefer full control? Following VNV manually is perfectly fine too.

Wabbajack + Nexus Premium

Recommended

Wabbajack is an automated modlist installer - it deploys the entire Viva New Vegas setup in minutes. With a Nexus Premium account, every mod downloads automatically while you grab a snack. This is the smoothest path.

Open the Wabbajack Guide ↗

Wabbajack - No Premium

Automated

The same automated installer, without a paid Nexus account. Everything works identically except you'll manually click "Download" for each mod as Wabbajack prompts you. A little more clicking, same great result.

Open the Wabbajack Guide ↗

Manual Install

Full Control

Prefer to build your setup mod-by-mod and understand every piece? Follow the full, step-by-step Viva New Vegas guide by hand. It takes longer, but you'll know your load order inside and out.

Open the Full VNV Guide ↗

Follow it to the end

Whichever route you choose, work through the official Viva New Vegas guide all the way to completion - a fully installed, launching game. Once VNV is done and running, return to this terminal to continue with Fauxvale Edition setup.
