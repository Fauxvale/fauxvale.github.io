Agua Fria - On the Topic of JSawyer

Before we move on to quality of life, a quick detour. Viva New Vegas ships with a mod called Agua Fria - JSawyer Redone, and whether you keep it is entirely a matter of taste. This page explains what it does so you can make that call. No action is required - if you're happy with it, just read on and continue to Part Two.

What Agua Fria does

Agua Fria is a modern, configurable reimplementation of JSawyer - the semi-official rebalance mod made by New Vegas director Josh Sawyer. It nudges the game back toward the harder, leaner balance he originally intended. The headline changes are:

▸ Lower starting Health and Carry Weight
▸ Slower leveling and a lower level cap
▸ Rebalanced Hardcore mode
▸ Assorted DLC and gear tweaks, including how the pre-order pack items are handed out and how the add-ons slot into a playthrough rather than dumping everything on you at once.

Why you might keep it - or not

Keep it if you want the "definitive," Sawyer-tuned survival experience: tighter resources, meaningful Hardcore mode, and a level curve that keeps the world dangerous for longer. Many players consider it the intended way to play.

Remove it if you'd rather keep vanilla balance - a higher level cap, faster leveling, and more forgiving Health and carry weight - or if you simply don't want a rebalance layer on top of your other mods. There's no wrong answer; it's your playthrough.

Option A

Keeping it

Nothing to do. Leave Agua Fria enabled exactly as VNV set it up and head straight to Part Two - Quality of Life.

Option B

Removing it

Prefer vanilla balance? Follow the three steps below to disable Agua Fria and drop in a lightweight replacement for its DLC pacing, then continue to Part Two.

!

The steps below are only for players who want to remove Agua Fria. If you're keeping it, skip straight past them to "Up next."

  1. Disable Agua Fria in the Gameplay separator

    Open MO2 and scroll up into your VNV setup to the Gameplay separator. Find these two mods and untick both checkboxes to deactivate them:

    ▸ Agua Fria
    ▸ Agua Fria INI

    Leave them in place - unchecked is enough. No need to delete anything.

  2. Install DLCs At Your Own Pace

    Agua Fria also controls how the add-ons ease into your game. With it disabled, this mod restores that "let the DLCs arrive on your terms" behaviour - stopping the game from bombarding you with add-on quests and radio messages the moment you leave Goodsprings.

    DLCs At Your Own Pace

    Delays and gates the DLC intros so the add-ons integrate at a sensible pace. Grab the main FNV file.

    Open on Nexus ↗

    Same flow as before: on the Nexus page open the Files tab, click Mod Manager Download on the main file, then double-click the finished download in MO2's Downloads pane to install it (keep the default name).

  3. Place it below OneTweak and enable

    In your mod list, drag DLCs At Your Own Pace so it sits directly below OneTweak but Really Updated under your Fauxvale Edition separator, then tick its checkbox to activate it.
