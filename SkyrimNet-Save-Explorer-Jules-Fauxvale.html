    The Land, As Seen
    A synthesis of all 85 captures

    Taken together, the captures trace one continuous road through four bands of country. It begins in the pine-dark river valley around Riverwood: moss-covered trunks, fern-carpeted trails, a stone bridge over the ravine, and the warm interiors of the Sleeping Giant and the Riverwood Trader with their hanging herbs and horn-mounted candles. From there the lens turns cold, climbing past the Guardian Stones and up snow-choked switchbacks to Bleak Falls Barrow, which the model reads again and again as something skeletal: collapsed archways "like the ribs of a long-dead beast," staircases leading to nowhere, blood-stained floors and bodies inside the tower.

    The middle of the record belongs to the Whiterun tundra, a wide brown-and-gold plain of dry grass, weathered boulders, and windmills, stitched together by cobblestone roads and signposts pointing to every corner of the province. The city itself appears in layers, from the fortified gate approach through the market square and Warmaiden's up to the braziers and turquoise stream of the Wind District and the timbered vault of Dragonsreach. Smoke over the Western Watchtower and a pair of massive skeletons at a nearby lair mark where the peace thins out.

    Then the road runs east and south: Valtheim's towers glowering over the White River chasm, the walled forest crossroads at Fort Amol with its sign for Ivarstead, Riften, and Windhelm, and a sudden change of season into the Rift, where every capture is thick with golden birch leaves, from Sarethi Farm's hay-strewn yard to the bridge into Ivarstead. The record ends in white: ten consecutive captures of the 7,000 Steps, each a snow-buried shrine or carved pillar glowing faintly blue in the frost, climbing until the stone face of High Hrothgar closes over the sky, and one final frame inside its dim monastic halls. The lone outlier is Clockwork Castle, all rusted pipes and dark ceremonial corridors, which belongs to no road at all.
